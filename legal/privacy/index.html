Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chris Ryle Insurance Services, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you visit or use our website (the "Service"), and explains the choices you have.

Interpretation and Definitions

"Personal Data" means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual. "Service" refers to our website. "Cookies" are small files placed on your device by a website, containing details of your browsing history.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

  • Personal Data. While using the Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Usage Data. Usage Data is collected automatically, including your device's IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on pages, and other diagnostic data.
  • Tracking Technologies and Cookies. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (including Google Analytics) to track activity on our Service and hold certain information, so we can improve and personalize your experience.

Use of Your Personal Data

Chris Ryle Insurance Services, LLC may use Personal Data for purposes including: to provide and maintain the Service; to manage your requests for insurance quotes, guidance, or appointments; to contact you by phone, email, text message, or mail regarding coverage options and services you've requested; to comply with legal obligations; and to improve our Service and offerings.

Sharing Your Personal Data

We may share your information with insurance carriers and service providers to process your request, with business partners to offer you certain products or services, and when required to comply with the law, protect our rights, or as part of a business transfer (such as a merger or sale of assets). We do not sell your personal information.

Retention of Your Personal Data

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, and to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Security of Your Personal Data

The security of your Personal Data is important to us, but no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your Personal Data. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Chris Ryle Insurance Services, LLC through the contact information provided on this website.
